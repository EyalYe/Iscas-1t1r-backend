\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}
A post-fabrication-tunable time-domain read for 1T1R RRAM PiM has been co-verified to a
tape-out-ready 64$\times$64 macro on IHP SG13G2. Its simulated budget is a design requirement, not
a verdict: the single-row read works at IHP's
median HRS, and 8-row NOR and majority require the deep-reset state with a spread of 0.16
(eight rows) or 0.33 (two), which a deeper reset or a tighter verify provides. Silicon must first measure that spread.
